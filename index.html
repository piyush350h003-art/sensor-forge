About Sensor Forge

Many small and mid-size factories still run machines they cannot see inside. Sensor Forge exists to change that with systems sized and priced for SMEs. We start from your problem, such as a motor that overheats, a line that stops unnoticed or a tank that overflows, and build the device that solves it. Our vision is that every workshop can afford to monitor its own equipment.

Our solutions

Each system is designed for your machines, not picked from a catalogue.

Customized sensor systems

Temperature, vibration, level, current, proximity and flow sensing, matched to what you need to know.

Industrial automation

Automate repetitive start, stop, count and sort tasks to cut manual effort and errors.

Equipment monitoring

Watch machine health and get alerts before a small fault becomes downtime.

Sensor-based control

Closed-loop control that acts on sensor readings, like cutting power on overheating.

Custom electronics and IoT

Purpose-built boards and connected dashboards you can check from your phone.

Our process

From first conversation to long-term support.

  1. Requirement analysisWe visit, listen and define what needs measuring or controlling.
  2. System designWe choose sensors, electronics and a layout that fits your budget.
  3. Development and prototypingWe build and test the device before it reaches your floor.
  4. InstallationWe install on site and train your team to use it.
  5. Maintenance and supportTechnical support and annual maintenance contracts (AMC) keep it running.
